Read before relying on it
  • 87% is the presenter's claim, not a measured probability for any match. No evidence for it was available to check. Every expected-return figure here is conditional on the probability set above.
  • Passing all four checks does not show a bet is profitable. The rules are filters. They have not been back-tested here, and the matches they pick are correlated (strong attacks, recent goals).
  • Adding two teams' scoring averages is a screening shortcut, not an expected-goals model. It ignores opponent strength, home and away, injuries, rotation and game state.
  • Small samples: early-season averages, five recent matches and a handful of head-to-head meetings, sometimes years apart with different squads, all carry wide error.
  • Ambiguities in the instructions: how many past meetings count and the minimum number (app settings here); whether "this season" means league play only (Goalmark uses the fixture's own competition); how to treat matches with extra time (regulation score only).
  • Prices: the implied probability includes the bookmaker's margin. The Odds API's soccer totals are taken to settle on 90 minutes plus stoppage time; check the house rules for your market.
  • Coverage: only fixtures both providers cover on your plans, in mapped competitions. This is never every game a sportsbook offers.
  • Goalmark never places bets.

Data connections

Recommended. Your keys stay in environment variables on the machine running the relay and never reach the browser. Start it with:

GOALMARK_APIFOOTBALL_KEY=your-key \ GOALMARK_ODDSAPI_KEY=your-key \ node goalmark/server.mjs

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8787. To use it from your phone on the same Wi-Fi, add HOST=0.0.0.0 and GOALMARK_ACCESS_TOKEN=a-long-secret, then enter that token below.

Keys are sent from this browser straight to each provider. A provider may refuse browser requests (CORS); use the relay if so.
